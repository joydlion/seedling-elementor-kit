How it works

Four steps, from first call to first invoice.

Every partnership grows the same way. Your team stays in charge of referrals, and we take care of the rest.

  1. 1.

    Partner

    Your school or district reaches out, and we talk through what your students need.

  2. 2.

    Identify need

    Your team identifies students and shares referrals through your usual process.

  3. 3.

    Match and onboard

    We size the support, match licensed clinicians and handle their onboarding.

  4. 4.

    Serve and invoice

    Clinicians work with your students, and we handle billing with your school.

FAQ

Questions schools ask us.

Short answers here. More detail, and compliance documents when they are ready, live on one page.

Read the Compliance and FAQ page

Do you work with families directly?

No. We work with school systems, districts and institutions. Students are served through their school.

What do you handle for our school?

We handle clinician sizing and matching, onboarding and billing. Your team keeps identifying need and assigning referrals.

Does this website collect student information?

No. None of our forms ask for student names or student information, and we ask that you don't include any.
